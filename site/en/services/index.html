Services

Five directions we have been working in since 2011. Each page covers what is included, how the work goes, what drives cost and schedule, and the projects where we have done it.

  • Museum concept design — From “why does this museum exist” to an exhibition design ready to be built.
  • Interactive exhibition design — An exhibition the visitor does not read but explores.
  • Interactive museum equipment — A screen, a model or a vitrine tells nobody anything on its own. We build the equipment around the story, not the story around the equipment.
  • Technical fit-out for museums — We write and audit the specification, put together the equipment list, and answer for all of it working as one system.
  • Interactive exhibition stand — A stand people stop at — and stay at long enough for someone to talk to them.
  • Holographic showcases and volumetric displays — An object that looks impossible — and therefore stops the crowd.
  • Projection mapping and interactive scale models — Physical form with information laid over it: a territory, a plant, an object.
  • Interactive showroom and brand space — A space where the product is shown through action, not through a brochure.
