Russian Industry

Interactive installations · Russia Expo

ClientVizhu · Department of Industry
Timeline2 months
Year2023

The goal

The country has ambitious plans for shipbuilding: a new generation of civilian vessels, breakthrough engineering, the modernization of the industry and the training of its specialists. All of it had to reach the wide audience of Russia Expo — spectacular and easy to follow.

The solution

We built a fully immersive exhibition. Visitors examine the flagship projects and the new ships in three dimensions, take them apart unit by unit and see where the industry’s innovation actually sits. Complex technology turns into a clear, gripping story — one the visitor lives through firsthand.
