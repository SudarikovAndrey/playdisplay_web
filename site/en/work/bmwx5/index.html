BMW X5

Car configurator

ClientBMW China
Timeline1 month
Year2016

The goal

Equip the sales office with an interactive tool for choosing and configuring a car.

The solution

With virtual reality, a touchscreen and a holographic installation, prospective buyers could explore the interior and exterior of the BMW X5, choose wheels, pick a body color and select cabin trim materials. The result could be viewed inside a futuristic garage or out on a road across the desert.

What the app does

  1. Presentation in VR, on a touchscreen and as a hologram
  2. Choice of wheels, body paint and interior trim
  3. Viewing the car inside a futuristic showroom or on a road across the desert
  4. Every light is interactive; doors open; you can see the cabin through the driver’s eyes
