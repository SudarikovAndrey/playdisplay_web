COALCO Dry Port

Interactive installation and 3D film

ClientCoalco Group
Timeline2 months
Year2016

The goal

The dry port is on such a scale that it only makes sense shown whole: how this new transport and logistics hub outside Moscow is put together, how its key systems work, and how it changes freight across the region.

The solution

This is not a film but a step-by-step installation under the viewer’s control. You drive the presentation yourself, examine the hub’s key points in 3D graphics with a full 360° view, and see the architecture, the traffic and the rhythm of the port at work — along with its geopolitical role on a three-dimensional world map.
