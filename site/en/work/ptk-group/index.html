Exhibition installation

ClientPTK Group
Timeline2 months
Year2019

The goal

PTK Group builds modern track machines — equipment for constructing, repairing and maintaining railways. Almost all of it shares one trait: it is long and drawn out.

The solution

We turned that trait into the format itself. The content was made for an ultra-wide screen on the booth that echoes the proportions of the machines. The display follows the shape of the product: each machine unfolds horizontally at full length, and its structure and specifications read clearly.
