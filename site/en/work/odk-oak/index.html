UEC UAC

Dynamic exhibition space · MAKS 2021

ClientUnited Engine Corporation (UEC) · Exposervice GmbH
Timeline3 months
Year2021

The goal

MAKS, the International Aviation and Space Salon, is one of the world’s major aerospace events. United Engine Corporation (UEC), Russia’s leading developer of aircraft engines, was presenting its pavilion there.

The solution

For the UEC booth we built a multimedia immersion into the world of aircraft engine manufacturing. A complex screen setup, one shared rhythm across every image and a set of interactive experiences turn the pavilion into a single living space where the technology reads at a glance.

  1. Project concept and design
  2. Engineering the multimedia solution, producing the graphics
  3. Integrating the graphics into the space: fitting, calibration, polish
  4. Installing and tuning the exhibits, on-site support
