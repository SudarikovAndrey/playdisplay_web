Cosmonautics and Aviation Center

An exhibition of 115 touchscreens · VDNH

ClientAviation and Cosmonautics Center
Timeline6 months
Year2016

The goal

Tell the history of spaceflight and rocketry through high-technology solutions.

The solution

We delivered an exhibition of 115 touchscreens and the installation The History of Human Spaceflight for the United Rocket and Space Corporation at the Cosmonautics and Aviation Center.

One information system

A single information system runs alongside every exhibit at the Cosmonautics and Aviation Center at VDNH. Visitors work it through the installation interface, and it gives them almost any fact about space and about what humanity has achieved beyond our planet.

  • 115 installations
  • 890 illustrations and diagrams
  • 149 visual themes
  • 2000+ pages of source material
  • 20 developers
  • 46 interactive 3D models
  • 67 videos and animations
  • 640 uniquely laid-out pages

For this task we developed a dedicated visual language and graphic interface.
