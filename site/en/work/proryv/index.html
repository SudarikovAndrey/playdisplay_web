Proryv

Rosatom · the presentation tunnel

ClientRosatom. Proryv (Breakthrough) project
Timeline2 months
Year2024

The goal

Build an installation that shows in plain visual terms how a nuclear power platform with a closed nuclear fuel cycle based on fast neutrons actually works.

The solution

An informational multimedia installation about the Proryv technologies. A complex screen setup and the tunnel effect create the sensation of moving through space and pull the viewer bodily into the story. The piece works as a visual ride and becomes the spot everyone photographs and films.

  1. Project concept and design
  2. Engineering, equipment selection and supply
  3. Building the tunnel structure and the decorative elements
  4. Assembly at the venue, dismantling, reuse

Outcome

  • 7,000 delegates over three days
  • 62 countries represented
  • 6 TV companies filmed the installation

At the IV Congress of Young Scientists the “Perpetual Motion” installation became one of the focal points for delegates and press alike. Over three days the Congress gathered more than 7,000 people from 62 countries, and the intricate principles of next-generation nuclear power found a form that was both clear and striking.

The space, drawn from the geometry of a nuclear fuel assembly, worked as an educational instrument and a strong visual image at the same time. Visitors got to know the closed nuclear fuel cycle, examined the technology at engineering level, and photographed and filmed the installation. Six television companies used it in their reports, and the photographs and video carried the story of the Proryv project on well beyond the Congress.
