Proryv

Rosatom · the presentation tunnel

ClientRosatom. Proryv (Breakthrough) project
Timeline2 months
Year2024

The goal

Build an installation that shows in plain visual terms how a nuclear power platform with a closed nuclear fuel cycle based on fast neutrons actually works.

The solution

An informational multimedia installation about the Proryv technologies. A complex screen setup and the tunnel effect create the sensation of moving through space and pull the viewer bodily into the story. The piece works as a visual ride and becomes the spot everyone photographs and films.

  1. Project concept and design
  2. Engineering, equipment selection and supply
  3. Building the tunnel structure and the decorative elements
  4. Assembly at the venue, dismantling, reuse
