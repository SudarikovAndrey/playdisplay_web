Property at the Scale of Being There

When a project has to be shown to a whole group with a sense of full immersion.

What it solves

  • Puts people inside the building before it exists.
  • Covers architecture, interiors and surroundings in one scenario.
  • Works both for a guided pitch and for self-guided viewing.

How it works

A large curved screen fills the field of view and renders the future project in real time. You can move across the site, step into interiors and overlay infographics.

I'm standing in the neighborhood before it's built.

Mechanics

  • Panoramic LED screen
  • Real-time 3D
  • Tablet or console control
  • Infographics
  • Companion touchscreens

Why this way

A big image changes the scale of perception: people stop reading the project as a picture and start judging distances, surroundings and atmosphere. Real-time control means a client's question gets answered on the spot.

Where it applies

Developer sales centers • corporate presentations • exhibitions • urban planning centers

What it can show

From a compact sales area to a full immersive hall. Day and night lighting, seasons, movement scenarios and personal routes are all possible.
