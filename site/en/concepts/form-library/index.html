Screens Become Architecture

When a space needs a form of its own and content has to escape the standard rectangle.

What it solves

  • Creates a recognizable visual language.
  • Merges architecture and media.
  • Lets you assemble different compositions from the same modules.

How it works

Screens of different sizes and geometries fold into faceted objects. From one particular viewpoint the content locks into a single image; as you move, it unfolds into a layered composition.

The image exists on the screen and in real space at once.

Mechanics

  • Custom-shaped screens
  • Anamorphic content
  • Modular structure
  • Kinetics — optional
  • Synchronized graphics

Why this way

An unusual form earns attention before a single frame plays. The anamorphic effect rewards the viewer for finding the right spot and makes moving through the space part of the experience.

Where it applies

Exhibitions • showrooms • retail • museums • offices • stages

What it can show

Scale, configuration, how much of it moves, the type of content and the number of perfect viewing points.
