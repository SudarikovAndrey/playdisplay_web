A Space Mission for a Real Crew

When you need a flagship attraction with a long scenario, repeat visits and strong group dynamics.

What it solves

  • Builds a full-scale adventure inside a museum or a center.
  • Binds participants together with shared responsibility.
  • Lets you release new missions and bring the audience back.

How it works

A group takes their seats aboard a spacecraft. Each person runs one system, and both the flight and the story depend on the crew's joint decisions.

We really are a crew — one person's mistake hits everyone.

Mechanics

  • Spherical panoramic screen
  • Individual consoles
  • Team puzzles
  • Motion platform
  • Spatial audio and physical set design

Why this way

A long experience holds attention through roles, a shared goal and a constant change in the kind of task at hand. Everyone carries their own responsibility, and the result exists only through coordination. A serial structure lets new missions launch and builds a returning audience.

Where it applies

Space museums • science centers • theme parks • future-facing visitor centres

What it can show

A spaceflight, a deep-sea expedition, running a city of the future, a research mission or a philosophical interactive performance.
