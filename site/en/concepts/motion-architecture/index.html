Every Step Becomes Part of the Message

When an everyday route should turn into a visual and narrative event.

What it solves

  • Turns a staircase into a full-fledged medium.
  • Links a person's movement to the unfolding of content.
  • Creates a strong image without taking up extra floor space.

How it works

Purpose-built LED screens cover the risers or the entire surface of a staircase. Text, graphics and artworks assemble into one image.

I'm climbing through a story, not just up the stairs.

Mechanics

  • Load-rated LED modules
  • Content built to the geometry of the steps
  • Anamorphic view
  • Response to movement — optional
  • Wayfinding integration

Why this way

A staircase already sets a sequence and a rhythm. Content can reveal itself step by step, guide people and resolve into one big image from a key vantage point. An everyday action becomes part of the narrative.

Where it applies

Museums • offices • universities • shopping centers • exhibitions • public buildings

What it can show

Wayfinding, the story of an organization, data, art, ad campaigns, game challenges and learning sequences.
