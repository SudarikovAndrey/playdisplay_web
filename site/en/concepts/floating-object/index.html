An Object That Looks Impossible

When a product or an exhibit has to own the room and be explored in three dimensions.

What it solves

  • Creates the illusion of a 3D object floating in air.
  • Lets people manipulate the model without a VR headset.
  • Combines a showstopper with a working product catalog.

How it works

A volumetric image appears inside a darkened prism. The visitor rotates the object, switches modes and opens further layers of information.

The object hangs in mid-air and answers to what I do.

Mechanics

  • Reflective holographic pane
  • Concealed screens
  • Projection onto floor or backdrop
  • Interactive console
  • 3D content

Why this way

The contrast between a dark enclosure and a glowing image hides every technical edge. Several synchronized visual layers build real depth, and interaction turns the effect from a display case into a tool for exploration.

Where it applies

Technology showrooms • museums • exhibitions • luxury retail • education centers

What it can show

Mechanisms, electronics, jewelry, architecture, anatomy, historical objects and digital art.
