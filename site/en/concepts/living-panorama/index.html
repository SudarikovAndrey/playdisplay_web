A Big Journey for a Big Audience

When dozens or hundreds of viewers have to step inside the same story at the same time.

What it solves

  • Delivers presence at scale.
  • Shows places and events at a scale that convinces.
  • Works as the climax of a route or as a standalone attraction.

How it works

A concave screen wraps the field of view and moves the audience into a virtual environment: a city, a historical era, a natural landscape or a world of technology.

The room disappeared and we were there.

Mechanics

  • Large concave LED screen
  • Panoramic content
  • Spatial audio
  • Interactive or linear scenario
  • Group viewing

Why this way

Peripheral vision is what makes presence feel real. A curved screen fills it and hides the edges of the image. That's why even a large group reads the content as a space rather than a film on a wall.

Where it applies

Museums • visitor centres • tourist centers • corporate showrooms • learning spaces

What it can show

Historical reconstructions, city journeys, expeditions into nature, industry, science, space, projects still to be built.
